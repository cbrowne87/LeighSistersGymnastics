Her Story

A Journey That Started at Five

Zaria-Leigh began her gymnastics journey at just five years old, and from the very beginning, she brought her own spark to the sport. Bold, energetic and always ready to move, Zaria has a personality that shines both in and out of the gym.

When she isn't flipping, tumbling or working on her next skill, you'll probably find her dancing. She loves music and movement, and that natural energy is a big part of who she is.

Gymnastics continues to teach Zaria discipline, courage and perseverance. With every training session and every competition, she is learning to challenge herself, build confidence and keep going—even when a skill doesn't come easily.

Her journey is still just beginning, and she is excited to see how far her hard work, determination and love for gymnastics will take her.
