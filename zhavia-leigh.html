Her Story

Focused on Learning & Growing

Zhavia-Leigh began her gymnastics journey at just five years old. From the start, she has brought a focused approach to the sport, with an amazing attention to detail and a genuine eagerness to learn and improve.

Zhavia loves learning new things. Whether she is working on a new skill, making a small correction or trying something for the first time, she enjoys the challenge of figuring it out and putting what she learns into practice.

She pays attention to the little details, and that precision can be seen in the way she approaches both training and competition.

Gymnastics continues to teach Zhavia discipline, confidence and perseverance. Each practice is another opportunity to learn, refine her skills and become a stronger gymnast.

Her journey is about continuing to learn, paying attention to the details, challenging herself and discovering just how much she is capable of.
A Special Milestone

CARIFTA 2026 National Team

Zhavia-Leigh's hard work has led to an exciting milestone in her gymnastics journey. She has qualified for the Level 1 National Team for the CARIFTA Gymnastics Championships 2026 in The Bahamas.
