Her Story

A Love for the Sport.

Keira-Leigh began gymnastics at six years old, and what started as a new adventure quickly grew into a genuine love for the sport. Dynamic, disciplined and determined, she brings energy to her gymnastics while continuing to challenge herself to learn, improve and reach new levels.

From her earliest competitions, Keira showed a natural ability to perform under pressure. At the 2023 Jamaica Classic, she was named the Level 1 Top Scorer, beginning an international competition journey that would take her from Jamaica to Costa Rica, CARIFTA and beyond.

As she progressed through the levels, Keira continued to build strong, confident performances across all four apparatus. Beam has become one of her standout events, while her competition results also show strength and consistency on bars, floor and vault.

Her journey has been marked not only by medals and championship titles, but by progression. From Level 1 to Level 2, then through Levels 3, 4, 5 and 6, each season has brought new skills, new challenges and new opportunities to represent Trinidad & Tobago.

For Keira, gymnastics is more than competition. It is a sport she genuinely loves—one that continues to teach her discipline, resilience, confidence and the value of working toward something one step at a time.

Now competing at Level 6 and having qualified to represent Trinidad & Tobago at the CARIFTA Gymnastics Championships 2026, Keira continues to pursue her journey with the same energy, discipline and love for gymnastics that started it all.
